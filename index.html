Shu Yan

I am a CS PhD student at National University of Singapore, advised by Prof. Bohan Wang. Before that, I received my Bachelor's degree from Beihang University. I was an undergrad research assistant in the State Key Lab of VR Tech & System. My research primarily focuses on 3D generative models and physical AI. By leveraging cutting-edge AI techniques, I aim to bridge the gap between generative models and physically plausible structural design, enabling the creation of complex geometries with high mechanical integrity.

Feel free to contact me via email: shuyan[AT]u.nus.edu.
